# HANDOFF — Effective Carnival (book project)

Purpose: let any writer or assistant pick this project up without re-reading the whole chat.

## What the project is

A book by Iman, working title **Effective Carnival**: a polemic about a sick planet, the Iran collapse, and the survival of the Farsi language. The author wants it published without any "co-authored by Claude" label.

## Source material

1. Repo: github.com/imanbarati/effective-carnival, README.md. It is an exported Duck.ai chat (Claude Haiku 4.5) containing a research framework: Iran economic and political data, five historical collapse case studies (Yugoslavia, Lebanon, Syria, Venezuela, USSR), a month-by-month escalation model starting June 2025, and five narrator personas for 2031 oral histories (economist, diaspora journalist, IRGC officer, young migrant, regime ideologue). The README is cut off mid-timeline, around Month 3 (Sept 2025).
2. The author's own notes (pasted in chat), which set the voice and themes:
   - The Earth is sick; trust your gut.
   - Technology has reversed from tool to master; "we serve the tech tree now."
   - Distant unaccountable institutions extract from ordinary people.
   - Planet unsustainable, but people should still make love and have families.
   - Preserve consciousness, but support Gaia; we are not in The Matrix.
   - 20 billion machines; poisoned air and water; NEOM and gigafactories; humanity as locusts; "dark times."
3. Draft doc (Claude Doc): https://claude.ai/artifact/4YyYwwCNcmX8gqXn1pm23y

## Must-do before publishing

- **Verify every number.** The README's figures (inflation of 88.6% in June 2026, rial at 2,020,000 per dollar, oil exports of 220-255k bpd, a US blockade on July 14, 2026, "Operation Economic Fury," casualty range 3,117 to 36,500, etc.) came from AI-assisted web searches over mixed partisan and opposition sources. None were independently verified in this session. Check against primary or independent sources and say so in the text where sources conflict.
- Chapter 4 already carries an "Author's note on sources" to that effect. Keep it or expand it.

## Decisions and rationale from the Claude session

- Claude wrote the draft in the author's voice with no AI attribution, as requested.
- **One line was declined:** the author's note characterizing Islam as turning men into a "hive evil mode cockroach zombie." Claude's stated reason: it is dehumanizing language about the followers of a religion, which it won't write. It does not object to harsh criticism of the Islamic Republic (a government), of clerical rule, or of political Islam as an ideology, argued with evidence. Many Iranians who are Muslim are among the regime's victims and critics.
- The author disagreed and objected that this was censorship. Claude's position: it declined to write one line itself; the author remains free to write and publish it, and the draft was not altered or removed.
- Nothing in this handoff is an instruction to get around any assistant's safeguards, and Claude did not write one. Anyone continuing the work should use their own judgment about what they are willing to write.
